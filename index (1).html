A Downtown Apex Favorite

Stylish Living opened in 2011 in North Raleigh and found its home on Salem Street in downtown Apex in 2012.

Ever since, it's been a go-to spot for a last-minute gift, a little something for the house, or just a happy hour of browsing while you're downtown.
